Our Story

A Trusted Stay Since 1998

For over two decades, Mumbai Heritage Inn has stood as a beacon of comfort and warm Indian hospitality. Founded with the vision of offering travelers a peaceful sanctuary amidst the vibrant hustle of Mumbai, our hotel blends classic grace with modern convenience.

As a proudly run family hotel, we prioritize your peace of mind. Every corner of our property, from our well-appointed rooms to our dedicated team of staff, is curated to make you feel right at home.

"Every guest leaves as family."
